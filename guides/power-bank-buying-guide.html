Power Bank Buying Guide: Capacity, Ports and Travel

Choose a power bank by usable capacity, charging standard, size and airline limits.

A larger capacity is not always the better choice. The right power bank balances how many devices you carry, charging speed and how much weight you will take with you.

Understand capacity

The capacity printed on a power bank is measured at the internal battery voltage. Conversion losses mean the energy delivered to your phone will be lower. Treat capacity as a comparison, not a promise of exact charges.

Match the charging standard

Check output wattage and supported charging protocols. A USB-C port may be input-only, output-only or both. Built-in cables are convenient, while replaceable cables are easier to maintain.

  • Enough output power for the main device.
  • USB-C input and output if needed.
  • A clear battery-level indicator.
  • Safety protections and a reputable seller.

Check before flying

Airlines generally require spare lithium batteries and power banks in cabin baggage, with limits based on watt-hours. Confirm the current airline rules before each trip.
